# CozyUni — World Bible v0.3

Status: **WORLD VISION LOCKED / WORLD PRODUCTION DEFERRED**

## 1. High concept

CozyUni is one connected cozy 3D universe for families and friends. The current production focus is the board-game collection. The persistent world is a later phase.

The long-term product has two layers:

1. **Family Game Table** — polished familiar board-game families rebuilt with CozyUni characters, presentation and light rule changes.
2. **Cozy World / Life Sim** — a persistent explorable world using the same residents, buildings, shops, parks, transport, regions and collectibles.

The world must not be built merely because many assets already exist. If/when it is built, it must justify itself as a real game layer.

## 2. Current production rule

**Do not build the world now. Build the games first.**

Reasons:
- gameplay quality is the current highest risk
- five polished board games are already enough production work
- a shallow world wrapper would consume time without adding meaningful retention
- existing assets can wait; they are not lost

The board-game architecture should not depend on the world being finished.

## 3. Future world quality bar

When CozyUni world development resumes, the target is **genuinely life-sim / Animal-Crossing-like in player experience**, while remaining an original CozyUni product.

That means the world should feel alive through systems, not just scenery.

Minimum qualities expected from a real world phase:
- player freely walks/explores as a chosen resident/avatar
- persistent place identity across sessions
- residents/NPCs present in the world rather than static menu mascots
- simple daily/ambient behaviors and movement
- usable shops and public spaces
- discoverable activities
- collectibles and reasons to revisit areas
- seasonal/event changes
- transport between meaningful regions
- social/friend visit potential where practical
- personalization/progression that changes what the player sees or owns
- game venues integrated naturally into the world

A decorative lobby with buildings around five game icons is **not enough**.

If the team cannot support a convincing life-sim layer, world production should remain deferred rather than shipping a fake version that wastes the asset library.

## 4. What the future world should NOT become by accident

Even with the higher quality bar, scope still needs control.

Do not assume the first world release requires:
- full interior for every building
- hundreds of unique NPC schedules
- deep farming/crafting from day one
- MMO-scale concurrency
- complex combat
- fully simulated economy

The goal is a coherent living cozy world, not feature parity with any existing franchise.

## 5. Existing asset library is reserved for the world phase

The already-created 3D asset families remain useful and should be preserved:
- animal cast
- shops and civic buildings
- landmark architecture
- roads, rail, bridges and transit
- harbor and mountain infrastructure
- parks and outdoor leisure
- sports/recreation structures
- vehicles and boats
- trees, bushes, flowers and ground nature
- food/produce
- village props
- urban services

These should eventually make regions feel dense and varied without needing to commission every environment from zero.

Until the world phase starts, do not render large amounts of additional world decoration without a specific gameplay/world-system need.

## 6. AI-first world production constraint

CozyUni has no traditional 3D art team. Most source assets are AI concept renders converted through image-to-3D workflows.

Therefore art feasibility is a product constraint.

Prefer assets that are:
- one clear object
- strong silhouette
- isolated and easy to segment
- simple ground contact
- chunky rather than thin/mechanically intricate
- readable from gameplay distance
- reusable across several locations

Avoid making future world systems depend on:
- elaborate bespoke interiors
- thin ropes/cables/chains as hero geometry
- complex articulated machinery
- transparent layered architecture
- crowds requiring many unique rigs
- assets that need heavy manual sculpting to become usable

## 7. Anchor location: Moonberry Village

Moonberry Village remains the identity anchor for the universe.

Recurring residents include Rabbit, Poppy Bear, Milo Dog, Lumi Cat, Mayor Pip Hedgehog and the wider Fox/Duck/Sheep/Dog/Koala/Panda cast.

When the world is eventually built, Moonberry should become a real playable place rather than only a board-game background.

## 8. Future regional structure

Working region directions:
- Moonberry Village — community core
- countryside / farms
- woodland / meadow
- lake / riverside
- town / urban quarter
- harbor / seaside
- mountain / cable-car region
- resort / tourist district

A region should exist because it adds activities, social value or meaningful world identity — not simply to consume more assets.

## 9. Board games and world integration later

The five current board games must work independently of the world.

Later, they can appear as natural activities/venues inside the life-sim:
- Ludo pavilion / family table
- Caro tables in café/park areas
- Chess garden or club
- Journey board at station/travel venue
- Tycoon board at market/civic venue

Players should still be able to launch games directly. World integration is immersion and discovery, not mandatory navigation friction.

## 10. Shared universe systems

Long-term shared systems may include:
- player profile
- character selection
- cosmetics
- achievements
- collection book
- seasonal calendar
- settings/accessibility/localization
- save/account layer
- room/lobby identity
- common audio/VFX language
- common results/reward presentation

These systems should be introduced only when the active game or future world phase actually needs them.

## 11. Tone and visual canon

- cozy, optimistic, readable
- playful competition rather than hostility
- soft rounded Style-D shapes
- controlled pastel palette
- strong silhouette at tablet/mobile distance
- restrained environment clutter
- buildings differ through massing/roof/facade/function, not recolor alone
- cute does not mean visually noisy

## 12. Decision rule

For now, all production questions are evaluated in this order:

1. Does this improve one of the five board games?
2. Does it create a reusable system for later games?
3. Is the required art reliable under the AI→3D pipeline?
4. Can it be tested before large content production?

If the answer is mainly "this will make the future world prettier," defer it.
